Questions, answered

Does moisture loss really change my portions?

Yes. A pot that loses 20 percent of its weight in cooking concentrates the same calories into fewer grams. Divide by the cooked weight and every tub matches. Divide by the raw weight and every tub is off.

What do I need besides the app?

A kitchen scale and the empty weight of your pan. Weigh the pan once, save it to your cookware list, and you never have to do it again.

Can I make tubs with different calorie targets?

Yes. Switch to calorie target mode, set a number for each tub, and MacroDivide shows the grams for each. It also tells you if you’ve allocated more calories than the recipe contains.

Why pay when free calculators exist?

A basic calculator divides one number by another. MacroDivide handles the part that goes wrong: pan weights, moisture loss, per-ingredient macros, saved recipes and printable labels, so you aren’t rebuilding the sum every Sunday.
