Privacy notice

This notice explains how Forma Labs (the operator of MacroDivide, “we” or “us”) handles information when you visit macrodivide.com, create an account, subscribe or ask for help.

Your recipes stay on your device.

The current app saves recipes, cookware weights and meal calculations in your browser’s local storage. These entries are not uploaded to our account database. Account and subscription information is stored separately so we can provide access.

1. Who is responsible?

Forma Labs is responsible for the personal information we use to operate MacroDivide. For privacy questions or rights requests, email support.macrodivide@proton.me. Stripe/Link is separately responsible for information it processes as merchant of record and payment provider.

2. Information we handle and why

  • Account information: your email address, account identifier, password authentication records, email-confirmation status and sign-in/session information. We use Supabase to create and secure your account. Passwords are handled by the authentication service; we do not receive them by email or store readable passwords.
  • Subscription information: payment-provider customer and subscription identifiers, trial use, subscription status, billing-period dates, cancellation status and relevant payment, refund or fraud events. We use this to grant access, prevent repeated free trials and reconcile billing. Card details are entered directly in Stripe/Link’s checkout, not stored by MacroDivide.
  • Technical information: our hosting and service providers process connection and request information such as IP addresses, timestamps, browser information and error/security logs to deliver and protect the site.
  • Service emails: your email address and message contents are passed to Brevo for account confirmation and password resets. Delivery, opening and link-interaction information may be recorded by the email provider to monitor email delivery and troubleshoot problems.
  • Support correspondence: information you choose to send to our support mailbox, used to answer questions and resolve account or product issues. Avoid sending medical records, card details or passwords.

Account and subscription processing is necessary to provide the service you request (performance of a contract). Security, abuse prevention and operational troubleshooting rely on legitimate interests in keeping the service reliable and secure. We also process records where needed to comply with legal obligations. Where consent is legally required for optional processing, we will ask before enabling it.

3. Browser storage and tracking

The app uses local storage for saved recipes and cookware and for authentication sessions. Signing out ends your active app session; it does not necessarily erase recipes saved on that device. You can remove saved items in the app or clear this website’s browser storage. Clearing storage, changing browser or changing domain can remove access to saved recipes; keep your own copies of anything important.

MacroDivide currently has no advertising pixels or third-party audience analytics installed. We do not sell your personal information. If optional advertising or analytics tracking is added, we will update this notice and obtain any required consent first. Stripe/Link’s checkout has its own privacy and cookie information.

4. Providers and sharing

We use the following services only as needed to operate MacroDivide:

  • OVHcloud: website hosting and associated technical logs.
  • Supabase: account authentication, subscription database and backend access checks. The configured project region is Ireland.
  • Stripe/Link: checkout, recurring billing, tax handling, subscription management and fraud prevention. As merchant of record, it also processes purchase information under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Brevo: transactional account emails and email delivery information.
  • Proton: our support mailbox and correspondence.
  • esm.sh: delivery of the browser authentication software; requesting it exposes normal connection information such as IP address to that service.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to handle legal claims or to protect users and the service. We do not send your locally stored meal entries to advertising platforms.

5. International processing

Although the account database is configured in Ireland, providers and their subprocessors may process information elsewhere, including outside the EEA or UK. Where required, transfers must use an applicable adequacy decision, standard contractual clauses or another lawful safeguard. Contact us to ask about the safeguards relevant to your information. Provider details are available in the Supabase data processing addendum and Brevo privacy policy.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep account and subscription records while needed to provide and secure your account. If you request account deletion, we remove or anonymise records that are no longer needed, while retaining records required for billing, tax, fraud prevention or legal claims for the applicable legal or claims period. Provider logs and backups follow the relevant provider’s retention and backup cycles and may expire later than active records. Support messages are retained while needed to resolve your request and related disputes.

Locally saved recipes remain in your browser until you delete them or clear storage. Deleting your MacroDivide account does not automatically erase local browser storage, cancel every payment-provider record or remove records Stripe must retain. Cancel your subscription separately before requesting account deletion.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw consent for any consent-based processing without affecting earlier lawful use. Email our support address to make a request; we may need proportionate verification of your identity. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. These rights are subject to applicable legal exceptions.

Email, authentication and billing information is required to create and maintain a subscribed account. Without it we cannot provide account access. MacroDivide does not use your meal entries to make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

8. Security, age and updates

We use HTTPS, provider-managed authentication and server-side subscription checks. No system can guarantee absolute security. MacroDivide is offered to adults aged 18 and over and is not designed for children. We will update this notice when our processing changes and communicate material changes through the service or account email where appropriate.
